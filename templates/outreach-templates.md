# Albert School — Partnership Outreach Templates

Skeletons for first-touch and follow-up emails. Every bracketed field must be
personalized from verified research — never send a template as-is. Keep the
final email under 150 words.

Signature: the sender's real name, role at Albert School, and email. The
agent must never invent a signature — take it from the user.

---

avec Mines Paris – PSL et soutenue notamment par Xavier Niel, Bernard

[Une phrase reliant le hook à la proposition : business case sur une

Auriez-vous 30 minutes dans les prochaines semaines pour en parler ?

Bien cordialement,
[Signature]

## EN — First touch (generic, to adapt)

**Subject:** [Company] × Albert School — [3–5 word angle]

Dear [First Last],

[One-sentence personalized hook: recent announcement, data/AI initiative,
public statement — with the specific fact.]

I'm reaching out on behalf of Albert School, founded in 2022 in partnership
with Mines Paris – PSL and backed by entrepreneurs including Xavier Niel,
Bernard Arnault, and Rodolphe Saadé. We train hybrid data/AI × business
profiles.

[One sentence connecting the hook to the proposal: a student business case
on a real [Company] problem, privileged access to our students for
internships and hiring, data/AI upskilling for your teams…]

Would you have 30 minutes in the coming weeks to explore this?

Best regards,
[Signature]

---

## Angle variants (swap into the proposal sentence)

- **Business case:** "Our student teams take on a real, scoped data problem
  from your business over 4–6 weeks, supervised by faculty — several
  partners use this as a low-commitment first collaboration."
- **Talent pipeline:** "Partners get privileged access to our students for
  apprenticeships (alternance), internships, and graduate hiring in data,
  AI, and business roles."
- **Executive education:** "We design data & AI upskilling programs for
  partner companies' managers and teams, drawing on the Mines Paris – PSL
  faculty."
- **Strategic partnership:** "Beyond one-off projects, partners sponsor
  chairs, join juries, teach, and build a visible presence with a
  generation of data-native talent."

---

## Follow-up (≥ 7 days later, max 2 per prospect)

Keep to 3 sentences: reference the first email in half a sentence, add ONE
new element (a new proof point, a relevant partner example, a new hook),
repeat the 30-minute ask. Never guilt-trip ("I haven't heard back…").
